# Written takeaways

Dataset: Seaborn **Tips** sample dataset (244 restaurant transactions).

1. Most meals have total bills below $25 (77.0% of the 244 observations).
2. The median tip is highest on Sun ($3.15) in this sample.
3. Total bill and tip have a positive linear association (Pearson r = 0.68); this is association, not proof of causation.
4. The strongest numeric correlation is between total_bill and tip (r = 0.68).
5. Average tip is highest on Sun ($3.26) and lowest on Fri ($2.73).

## Questions explored
1. How are restaurant bills distributed?
2. Do tip amounts differ by day?
3. Does a larger bill tend to come with a larger tip?
4. Which numeric variables move together?

## Interpretation note
These are descriptive patterns in this sample. Correlation does not establish causation, and the dataset should not be treated as a representative sample of all restaurants.
